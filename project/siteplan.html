Getting Around Port Harcourt

A Commuter's Guide — Website Planning Document

Site Name

Site Name: Getting Around Port Harcourt

Optional domain: phcommuterguide.com

I chose this name because it is direct and describes exactly what the site does: help people understand how to move around Port Harcourt using public transport. It is easy to remember and clearly targets commuters — residents, students, and visitors — rather than tourists looking for general travel information.

Site Purpose

The site provides a clear, organized guide to public transport in Port Harcourt, Rivers State. It explains the different transport options (keke tricycles, buses, taxis, ride-hailing apps, and water transport), lists major routes and landmarks used for navigation, gives typical fare ranges and payment tips, and shares safety and etiquette advice. The goal is to give newcomers the confidence to use the system and to give regular commuters useful, practical reference information in one place.

Scenarios

  • How much should I expect to pay for a keke ride from Rumuokoro to Mile 1, and should I have exact change ready?
  • What are the busiest travel times to avoid in Port Harcourt, and how do I manage commuting during the rainy season?
  • Which transport option is safest and most affordable for a student traveling daily from Choba to Trans-Amadi?

Color Schema

The palette is inspired by Port Harcourt's greenery and its riverside, oil-city identity. The colors below are applied to this document already.

Deep Palm Green (#0f6b4f)

Used for headings, header background, and primary accents.

River Blue (#1f3a5f)

Used for body text, section borders, and the footer background.

Taxi Yellow (#ffb703)

Used for accent highlights, links on hover, and callout backgrounds.

Cloud White (#f4f7f5)

Used for page and card background color.

Typography

  • Montserrat (600, 800) — used for all headings, the site name, and the navigation. It is bold, modern, and highly legible at large sizes.
  • Source Sans 3 (400, 600) — used for body paragraphs, lists, and captions. It is a clean, readable sans-serif designed for on-screen reading.
