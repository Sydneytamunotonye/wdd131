Discover Nigeria

Nigeria is the most populous country in Africa, a vibrant nation on the Gulf of Guinea known for its rich culture, diverse languages, and booming entertainment industry. From the bustling streets of Lagos to the savannas of the north, Nigeria offers a blend of tradition and modernity that draws visitors from around the world.

Nollywood, Nigeria's film industry, is one of the largest in the world, and its music scene — from Afrobeats to highlife — has a global following. The country is also home to stunning natural landmarks and warm, welcoming people.
