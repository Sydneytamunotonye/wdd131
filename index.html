Port Harcourt, Nigeria

Port Harcourt is a city in Nigeria, located in the southern part of the country. It is the capital of Rivers State and is known for its oil industry and vibrant culture. The city has a rich history and is home to many ethnic groups, including the Ikwerre, Ogoni, and Ijaw people. Port Harcourt is also a hub for education and has several universities and research institutions. The city is known for its lively music scene, with many local artists gaining national and international recognition. Overall, Port Harcourt is a dynamic and diverse city that offers a unique blend of culture, history, and modernity.
